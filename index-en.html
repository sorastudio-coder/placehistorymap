Place History Map

View your past movement history on a map

About Place History Map

Place History Map uses location information recorded by your iPhone to display your past movement history on a map.

Search for a place on the map to see the dates when location information was recorded around that place. Select a date to view the movement route for that day on the map.

Key Features

  • Displays movement history using location information recorded by your iPhone
  • Displays movement history on a map
  • View movement history by date
  • Search for places on the map
  • Shows dates with movement history around a searched place
  • Displays the movement route for a selected date
  • Set the retention period for movement history
  • Enable or disable movement history recording

Location Information

Place History Map uses location information to record movement history and display it on a map.

To record movement history, enable "Record Movement History" in Settings.

Privacy

Movement history and other data are managed on your device to provide the app's features.

Place History Map does not send movement history or other app data to external servers.

For more information, please see the Privacy Policy .
