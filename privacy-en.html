Privacy Policy

SoraStudio ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy when using Place History Map ("the App") and handles location information and related data appropriately.

1. Use of Location Information

The App uses location information to record movement history and allow you to view your past movement history on a map.

The App also uses location information embedded in photos and videos to display where they were taken on a map.

This location information is used to provide the movement history and photo and video location features of the App.

2. Data Storage

Movement history and other data recorded by the App are managed on your device to provide the App's features.

Photos and videos are accessed through your device's photo library to display their locations. To view photo and video locations, the files must contain location information.

We do not send or store your location information, movement history, or information related to your photos and videos on our own external servers.

3. Sharing with Third Parties

We do not provide your location information, movement history, or other information recorded in the App to third parties.

4. Location and Photo Library Permissions

To record movement history with the App, you must allow the App to access your iPhone's location.

To record movement history, please allow appropriate location access in your iPhone's settings.

To view photo and video locations on the map, you must also allow the App to access your photo library. The photos and videos the App can access depend on the permissions you grant in iOS.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may change this Privacy Policy when necessary.

Any changes to this Privacy Policy will take effect when the revised policy is posted on this page.
